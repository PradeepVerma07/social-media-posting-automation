# CI360 Troubleshooting & Error Resolution

Guide to diagnosing and resolving common operational issues.

---

## 1. n8n Webhook Timed Out (HTTP 504 / Connection Refused)

- **Cause**: The self-hosted n8n instance is either stopped, blocked by firewall, or the `N8N_BASE_URL` is unreachable.
- **Solution**:
  1. Open the **Automations** tab in CI360.
  2. Click **Test n8n Bridge** to verify network latency.
  3. Confirm your n8n container is running (`docker ps`) and accessible over HTTPS.
  4. Ensure `N8N_TIMEOUT_MS` in `.env` is set to at least 15000 (15s).

---

## 2. Webhook Callback Rejected (401 Unauthorized / Invalid Signature)

- **Cause**: The HMAC SHA-256 signature generated by n8n does not match the server's `N8N_WEBHOOK_SECRET` or the request timestamp is stale (>300 seconds).
- **Solution**:
  1. Verify the `N8N_WEBHOOK_SECRET` matches across both `.env` and your n8n environment variables.
  2. Check server time synchronization (NTP). Ensure system clocks are in sync.

---

## 3. Hostinger Database Connection Limit Exceeded

- **Cause**: Shared hosting plans limit concurrent MySQL connections per user.
- **Solution**:
  - In `server/src/config/db.ts`, ensure `connectionLimit` is set to `5` or lower.
  - Check that all connections are properly released back to the pool after query execution.

---

## 4. Post Schedule Collisions

- **Cause**: Multiple posts are queued to release at the exact same minute on the same brand channel.
- **Solution**:
  - Open the **Content Calendar** and look for amber collision badges.
  - Stagger posts by at least 15 to 30 minutes to maximize audience engagement.
